A Rite of the Hollow Wood

How to Play

1. Flip for first roll. A coin decides who rolls first each summoning phase. Then pass the device back and forth — one roll each — tapping the ground near the dice.

2. Lock a target. The first stone creature you roll locks in as your target for that creature type. Keep rolling until you land its shadow — that's when the pair completes and joins your roster, with the shadow's power (0–3) attached. Then you're free to lock a new target on your next stone.

3. Declare battle. Once you've completed at least one pair — and your opponent has too — "Declare Battle" becomes available. There's no fixed roll count — declare whenever you're ready (rolls cap out at 11 each if you keep going). Whoever declares attacks first.

4. Attack. Each creature attacks exactly once per battle: one roll of the attack d8, plus its power (0–3) added straight onto that roll as a flat bonus. Rolling 🔆 counts as 4 points before power is added. Extra Swing cards can grant additional attack rolls.

5. Defend. If the defender has any creature summoned, they roll the same d8 back for every attack roll. A numeral does nothing. 🔆 reflects half the attacker's total (rounded up, minimum 1) right back at them. Either way, the defender still takes the attacker's full damage. Extra Guard cards can grant additional defense rolls against each attack.

6. Back and forth. Once every creature on both sides has attacked, if nobody's been knocked out, HP and rosters carry over and you return to summoning for more creatures before declaring again — same round.

7. Best of three. A round ends only on a KO. When it does, everything wipes for a fresh round — new rosters, HP back to 20. First to 2 round wins takes the game.

8. Cards. Everyone starts holding 2, and draws more as the match goes — once at the start of every phase, plus a bonus draw whenever you play a card (costs your next roll if it's during summoning) and one more every 11th roll. Hand caps at 3; drawing past that forces a discard. One card per turn, and only on your own turn. Browse the full 20-card deck from the title screen's Card Gallery.
