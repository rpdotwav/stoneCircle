How to Play

1. Flip for first roll. A coin decides who rolls first each summoning phase. Then pass the device back and forth — one roll each — tapping the ground near the dice.

2. Lock a target. The first stone creature you roll locks in as your target for that creature type. Keep rolling until you land its shadow — that's when the pair completes and joins your roster, with the shadow's power (0–3) attached. Then you're free to lock a new target on your next stone.

3. Declare battle. Once you've completed at least one pair, "Declare Battle" becomes available. There's no fixed roll count — declare whenever you're ready (rolls cap out at 11 each if you keep going). A coin flip then decides who attacks first.

4. Attack. Each creature attacks exactly once per battle. Its power (0–3) is literally how many times it rolls the attack d8 — power 0 can't deal damage, power 3 rolls three times, summed together. Rolling 🔆 on the attack die counts as 4 points.

5. Defend. If the defender has any creature summoned, they roll the same d8 back for every attack roll: a number sends that much damage right back at the attacker, and 🔆 reflects half the attacker's roll (rounded down). The defender still takes the attacker's full damage regardless.

6. Back and forth. Once every creature on both sides has attacked, if nobody's been knocked out, HP and rosters carry over and you return to summoning for more creatures before declaring again — same round.

7. Best of three. A round ends only on a KO. When it does, everything wipes for a fresh round — new rosters, HP back to 20. First to 2 round wins takes the game.
