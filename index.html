How to Play

1. Roll, alternating. Pass the device back and forth — one roll each, tapping "Cast All Three" — until both players reach 11 rolls.

2. Match your creatures. Rolling both a stone and a shadow of the same creature during your 11 rolls claims that creature for your roster. The shadow's power (0–3) becomes that creature's power in battle.

3. Coin flip. Once both players finish rolling, a coin decides who attacks first this round.

4. Battle. Turns alternate. Each creature can attack twice before it retires. A creature's power (0–3) sets how many times it rolls the d8 for damage — power 0 rolls once, power 3 rolls four times, summed together.

5. Win the round. Knock the other player from 20 to 0, or — if both sides run out of attacks first — whoever has more points left wins the round.

6. Best of three. Every round starts from a blank slate — both rosters wipe clean and you roll a fresh 11 each before the next round begins. First to 2 round wins takes the game.
